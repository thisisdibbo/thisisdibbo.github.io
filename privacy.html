Dibbo Autopilot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Dibbo Autopilot is a personal tool used only by Md. Mahin Rahman to upload videos to his own YouTube channel, Dibbo Builds, through the YouTube Data API.

The app only requests permission to upload videos to the channel of the account that signs in. It does not read, store, sell or share any personal data, and it is not offered to anyone else.

The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube's terms also apply: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
